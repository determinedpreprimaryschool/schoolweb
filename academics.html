Academics

Pre-primary and primary education built on strong early foundations.

Pre-primary and primary, under one roof

Determined Primary School offers both pre-primary and primary education, with particular importance placed on building strong foundations in the early years. That early groundwork — language, number sense, and confidence — shapes how a child moves through every later stage of school.

What we focus on

Literacy & numeracy

The core building blocks of reading, writing, and working with numbers, taught with patience and repetition suited to each learner's pace.

Communication skills

Children are encouraged to express themselves clearly and confidently, in the classroom and beyond it.

Creative thinking

Lessons make room for imagination and curiosity alongside structured learning.

Problem-solving

Learners are guided to work through challenges themselves, building the kind of independence that lasts beyond a single lesson.

How we teach

Learning is supported through practical classroom activities, visual teaching materials, and lessons designed to keep children engaged and participating. We recognise that children learn in different ways, and we aim to offer the range of experience that lets every learner reach their full potential.
