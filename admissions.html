Admissions

How to join Determined Primary School, from enquiry to enrolment.

Who can apply

We welcome children from pre-primary age (around three years old) through primary level, up to about sixteen years old. Applications are open for pre-primary through Grade 7.

What to bring

  • Birth certificate or under-five card
  • Previous school report, if your child has attended school before
  • Guardian identification
  • Passport-sized photos of the child

How admissions work

  1. Enquire. Reach out by WhatsApp or phone to let us know you're interested.
  2. Visit the school. Come see the classrooms and meet the team in Maramba.
  3. Submit an application. Fill in our short application form with your child's details.
  4. Placement discussion. We talk through the right grade and any specific needs your child has.
  5. Enrolment. Once everything is confirmed, your child joins their class.

Fees

Determined Primary School charges school fees. Current fee information is available on request — please contact us directly and we'll be glad to talk you through it.

Ready to apply?

Start the application whenever you're ready — it only takes a few minutes.
