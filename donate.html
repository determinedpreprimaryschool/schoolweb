Support the school

Your gift goes directly to the school — toward learning materials, classroom resources, teacher support, and keeping the building in good shape.

Why it matters

Determined Primary School grew out of one person's living room into a place serving hundreds of children, almost entirely through community effort. That same support — from people like you — is what keeps classrooms stocked with materials, teachers supported, and the building maintained.
